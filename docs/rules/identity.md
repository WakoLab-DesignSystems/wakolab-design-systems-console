# Identity

Console is a **terminal-inspired** system for dense, keyboard-friendly tools.

- **Type:** JetBrains Mono for the interface and headings (weight 600, no tracking), at 14 px for interface text and 16 px in inputs; DM Sans only for prose (leads and long descriptions).
- **Density:** rows of at least 44 px; compact headers, tools and chips.
- **Shell:** a sticky top bar on the page background with a bottom border; from 64em, a sticky filter **rail** beside the collection; below that, filters fold into a toggled chip panel.
- **Collection as a table:** dense rows with a name, a summary and three metadata columns, a keyboard hint line (`/` search, `j` `k` move, Enter open) and a `›` marker on the focused row in the `accent` colour.
- **Geometry and depth:** square geometry (4 px controls, chips and covers, 6 px cards), **flat** surfaces (no card shadow in either mode) and a shadow only on overlays.
- **Motion:** short, 100 ms.
- **Brand:** the logo slot's text fallback is `›` followed by the product label.

Consumers may change any configurable value; these rules describe the defaults.
